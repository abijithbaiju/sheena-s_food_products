Purity in Every Jar

A Family Recipe, Shared With You

Sheenas Food Product began as a family kitchen tradition. Our Ammachede Achar — "grandmother's pickle" — was always the centrepiece of every meal, made with hand-pounded spices, cold-pressed oil and seasonal produce.

Today we bring that same recipe to your table in small batches. No mass production, no chemical preservatives, no synthetic colours — just honest, home-style pickling the way it has always been done.

Whether it's our fiery chicken pickle, tangy raw mango, or sun-cured lemon, every jar is sealed with care and delivered across India.
