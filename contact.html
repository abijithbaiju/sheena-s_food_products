Common Questions

Frequently Asked

What jar sizes do you offer?

We offer 100g, 250g, 500g and 1kg jars. All prices are calculated from our per-kg rates (Veg ₹400/kg, Non-veg ₹1500/kg).

Why do prices change?

Some ingredients are seasonal (like raw mango or certain spices), so prices may vary depending on season and availability.

How does the new-customer combo work?

New customers get 15% off a 3-jar box (250g each). You must choose either all veg or all non-veg jars — no mixing. Offer is one-time use per customer.

How are shipping charges calculated?

Shipping is added at checkout based on your delivery state: Kerala ₹70, other South Indian states ₹100, and the rest of India ₹140.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes — we ship all-India. Orders are dispatched within 2–3 working days in sealed glass jars.
